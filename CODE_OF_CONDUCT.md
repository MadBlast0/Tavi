# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Communicating professionally and assuming good faith where reasonable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized attention or advances, doxxing, threats, and publishing private information without permission.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, code, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

To report abusive or harmful behavior, contact the project maintainers privately using the contact method listed in the repository profile or README. Add a dedicated contact address or reporting process before publishing if one is available.

Maintainers will handle reports as fairly and confidentially as practical. They may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning or temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
